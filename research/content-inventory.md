## 2. Institutional Copy Inventory

### Vision Statement
> *"Every human being gets state-of-the-art medical treatment regardless of their background."*

### Mission Statement
> *"Our mission is to tirelessly strive towards eliminating disparities in healthcare by delivering state-of-the-art medical treatment to every individual, irrespective of their background. Through unwavering dedication, innovation, and compassionate care, we aim to ensure that every person receives equitable access to the highest standard of healthcare services, empowering them to lead healthy and fulfilling lives."*

### The Five Core Values
1. **Treating Everyone Equally:** Equal dignity, compassion, and respect regardless of socioeconomic status, caste, creed, or background, with subsidized/charitable care via ARDOR Care Foundation.
2. **International Collaboration:** Joint case panels and regular clinical reviews between American and Indian dual board-certified specialists to evaluate complex neurovascular, cardiovascular, and oncological cases.
3. **Healing Environment:** Calming, low-stress architecture with restricted daily appointment volumes to avoid chaos, overcrowding, and long wait times.
4. **Focused Comprehensive Service:** Deep subspecialty focus on stroke, heart attacks, cancer screening, and vascular health where early intervention saves lives.
5. **Preventive First Approach:** Proactive screening before irreversible disease manifestation.
