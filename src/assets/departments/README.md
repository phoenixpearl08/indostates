# Department Images & Banners
Location: `src/assets/departments/`

## Purpose
Visual banners and clinical suite images representing clinical, surgical, and diagnostic departments.

## Status
`[HOSPITAL TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL ASSETS]`

## Recommended Specifications
- **Header Banner:** 1200x500 px (2.4:1 ratio) or 1200x675 px (16:9), WebP format, < 150 KB
- **Directory Thumbnail:** 600x400 px (3:2 ratio), WebP format, < 80 KB
- **Naming:** `dept-[slug].webp` (e.g. `dept-cardiology.webp`, `dept-neurology.webp`)

## Centralized Mapping
Assets are registered in `src/assets/imageRegistry.ts` and referenced in `departments` data.
